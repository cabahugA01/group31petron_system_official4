Role

Staff

Staff members are responsible for monitoring inventory levels and submitting stock requests when items are running low.
  • ✓ View fuel & merchandise inventory
  • ✓ Monitor low stock alerts
  • ✓ Submit stock requests
  • ✓ View inventory history
  • ✓ View reports (read-only)
  • ✗ Cannot approve requests
  • ✗ Cannot adjust inventory
  • ✗ Cannot export reports
👔
Role

Manager

Managers have full operational control over inventory management including approvals, deliveries, stock-in, adjustments, and counts.
  • ✓ All Staff permissions
  • ✓ Approve/reject stock requests
  • ✓ Generate purchase orders
  • ✓ Receive & validate deliveries
  • ✓ Perform stock-in operations
  • ✓ Make inventory adjustments
  • ✓ Conduct inventory counts
  • ✓ Generate & export reports
👑
Role

Admin

Admins provide oversight through audit trails, reports, and the ability to rollback incorrect adjustments made by managers.
  • ✓ View all inventory (read-only)
  • ✓ Monitor all activities
  • ✓ View inventory count results
  • ✓ Full audit trail access
  • ✓ Rollback Manager adjustments
  • ✓ Generate all reports
  • ✓ System backup & restore
  • ✗ Cannot perform operations
