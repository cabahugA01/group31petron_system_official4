## 💾 Database Queries

### Tables Accessed
1. `merchandise_transactions` - Sales data
2. `job_orders` - Service orders
3. `job_order_parts` - Parts used in services
4. `users` - Staff information
5. `products` - Product catalog
6. `inventory` - Stock levels
7. `voided_transactions` - Cancelled records
8. `stations` - Station details

### Key Filters Applied
```sql

-- Job Orders: Only completed work
status IN ('Completed', 'Released', 'Verified')

-- Date Range: Within selected period
DATE(transaction_date) BETWEEN ? AND ?

-- Station: Admin's assigned station
station_id = ?
```
