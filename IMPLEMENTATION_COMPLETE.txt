📊 THE 8 SECTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

STANDARD SECTIONS (Managers also have these):
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. ✅ Merchandise Sales
   → Receipt No., Customer, Category, Product, Qty, Price, Amount, Staff
   → Footer: Total Merchandise Sales

2. ✅ Job Order / Service Sales
   → JO No., Customer, Vehicle, Service Type, Labor, Parts, Total, Mechanic
   → Footer: Total Service Income

3. ✅ Merchandise Products Used as Job Order Parts
   → JO No., Customer, Product, Category, Qty Used, Price, Cost
   → Footer: Total Parts Used & Cost

4. ✅ Payment Breakdown
   → Cash, GCash, Card, Charge Account
   → Transaction count and amount per method

7. ✅ Daily Collection Summary
   → Merchandise + Labor + Parts = Gross Sales
   → Minus Discounts = Net Collection


ADMIN-ONLY SECTIONS (Managers CANNOT see these):
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. 🔴 STAFF PERFORMANCE (ADMIN ONLY)
   → Staff Name, Merch Transactions, Job Orders, Sales, Collection
   → Purpose: Monitor individual staff productivity
   → Highlight: RED BACKGROUND

6. 🔴 INVENTORY IMPACT SUMMARY (ADMIN ONLY)
   → Product, Beginning Stock, Sold, Used in JO, Ending Stock
   → Purpose: Verify inventory movement accuracy
   → Highlight: BLUE BACKGROUND

8. 🔴 TRANSACTION AUDIT SUMMARY (ADMIN ONLY)
   → Total Transactions, Cancelled, Voided, Refunded counts
   → Purpose: Auditing and compliance
   → Highlight: ORANGE BACKGROUND
