# STAFF REPORTS MODULE - QUICK START GUIDE

## 🚀 Access the Module

**URL**: `public/staff_reports_complete.php`

**From Dashboard**: Click any report shortcut card in the "Reports & Add-ons" section

---

## 📊 Available Reports

### 1. Sales Reports
- **Daily Sales Summary**: View daily sales with payment method breakdown
- **Customer Transaction Linkage**: See which transactions are linked to customer profiles

### 2. Job Orders Reports
- **Job Order Tracker**: Track all job orders you created
- **Staff Performance Report**: View your daily performance metrics

### 3. Deliveries Reports
- **Fuel Deliveries**: View station fuel deliveries
- **Merchandise Deliveries**: Track deliveries you encoded
- **Inventory Movement**: See all inventory logs

### 4. Meter Reading Reports
- **Pump Reading Logs**: View all meter readings by date

### 5. Payments Reports
- **All Payments**: See all payment records
- **Unpaid**: Filter unpaid transactions
- **Pending**: View pending payments
- **Paid**: Show completed payments

### 6. Customer Reports
- **Customer List**: View basic customer profiles
- **Customer History**: See transactions linked to customers

### 7. Activity Reports
- **Staff Activity Log**: Your daily activity summary
- **Audit Trail**: Your audit log (own actions only)

---

## 🔍 How to Use

1. **Select Report Category**: Click category in left sidebar
2. **Choose Sub-Report**: Click specific report type
3. **Set Date Range**: Adjust "From Date" and "To Date"
4. **Apply Filters**: Click "Apply Filters" button
5. **View Data**: See results in table format
6. **Export**: Click "Excel" or "CSV" to download

---

## 📤 Export Options

- **Excel**: Formatted spreadsheet with Petron branding
- **CSV**: Plain text format for further processing

**Note**: You can only export your own data (staff-scoped)

---

## 🎯 Summary Cards

Each report shows key metrics at the top:
- Total counts
- Sum amounts
- Average values
- Performance indicators

---

## 🔒 Data Scope

- You see **only your own transactions**
- Data is **limited to your assigned station**
- Cannot view other staff members' data
- Cannot access admin-level information

---

## 💡 Tips

1. **Start with a short date range** (7-30 days) for faster loading
2. **Use Daily Sales Summary** for end-of-day reconciliation
3. **Check Activity Reports** to track your productivity
4. **Export data regularly** for your personal records
5. **Payment Reports** help identify outstanding balances

---

## ❓ Common Tasks

### View Today's Sales
1. Go to: **Sales Reports → Daily Sales Summary**
2. Set both dates to today
3. Click "Apply Filters"

### Check Your Job Orders
1. Go to: **Job Orders Reports → Job Order Tracker**
2. Select date range
3. Review status column

### Export Customer List
1. Go to: **Customer Reports → Customer List**
2. Click "Excel" or "CSV" button

### Review Your Activity
1. Go to: **Activity Reports → Staff Activity Log**
2. Select date range
3. See daily breakdown

---

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**No data showing?**
- Check your date range
- Verify you have transactions in that period
- Ensure you're logged in correctly

**Export not working?**
- Make sure there's data to export
- Try a different browser
- Check if pop-ups are blocked

**Wrong data?**
- Verify date range is correct
- Ensure correct sub-report is selected
- Refresh the page
