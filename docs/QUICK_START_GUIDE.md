# Job Orders Report - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 Getting Started in 3 Steps

### Step 1: Access the Report
1. Login to your Petron Station Management System
2. Look at the left sidebar menu
3. Find **"Reports"** section
4. Click **"Job Orders Reports"**

### Step 2: Select Your Filters
- **Report Date**: Choose the date you want to view (defaults to today)
- **Shift**: Select specific shift or view all shifts
  - Shift 1: 6:00 AM - 2:00 PM
  - Shift 2: 2:00 PM - 10:00 PM
  - Shift 3: 10:00 PM - 6:00 AM
  - All Shifts: Combined view
- Click the **"Apply"** button

### Step 3: View or Export
- **View on Screen**: Scroll through the report
- **Export Excel**: Click green "Export Excel" button
- **Export CSV**: Click blue "Export CSV" button
- **Export PDF**: Click red "Export PDF" button

---

## 📊 What You'll See

### Summary Cards (Top of Page)
Six colorful cards showing at-a-glance metrics:
- 📋 **Total Job Orders**: How many jobs for the day
- ✓ **Completed Jobs**: Successfully finished
- ⏳ **Pending/Active**: Still in progress
- 💰 **Unpaid Jobs**: Awaiting payment
- ₱ **Total Amount**: Total revenue
- 🚫 **Cancelled Jobs**: Rejected/voided

### Job Orders Table
Complete list with these columns:
- **Job Order ID**: Unique reference number
- **Customer Name**: Who requested the service
- **Service Type**: Type of work (Oil Change, Tire, etc.)
- **Parts/Materials**: Items used from inventory
- **Quantity**: How many pieces/liters
- **Unit Price**: Price per item
- **Total Amount**: Final cost
- **Payment Mode**: Cash, Credit, or Suki
- **Status**: Current stage (color-coded badges)
- **Staff Encoder**: Who created the job order
- **Remarks**: Additional notes

### Shift Summaries
Three boxes showing performance by shift:
- Total jobs per shift
- Total amount earned
- Completed count
- Cash collected
- Credit extended

### Overall Daily Summary
Combined totals across all shifts with complete breakdown

### Additional Summaries
Three specialized sections:
1. **Unpaid Job Orders** - Who still owes money
2. **Completed Job Orders** - Successfully finished work
3. **Cancelled Job Orders** - Rejected orders with reasons

---

## 🎨 Understanding the Color Codes

### Status Badges
- 🟢 **Green** = Completed (finished successfully)
- 🟡 **Yellow** = Pending (waiting to start)
- 🔵 **Blue** = In Progress (currently working on it)
- 🔴 **Red** = Cancelled (rejected or voided)

### Payment Badges
- 🟢 **Green** = Cash (paid in full)
- 🔵 **Blue** = Credit (to be paid later)
- 🟡 **Yellow** = Suki (regular customer credit)

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### "No Job Orders Found" Message
**Cause**: No job orders for selected date/shift
**Solution**: Try different date or select "All Shifts"

### Export Button Not Working
**Cause**: Browser popup blocker or permissions
**Solution**: 
1. Allow popups for this site
2. Check Downloads folder
3. Try different browser if issue persists

### Summary Cards Show Zero
**Cause**: No data for selected filters
**Solution**: Verify job orders exist in database

### Table Shows Wrong Data
**Cause**: Cache or old data
**Solution**: 
1. Click "Apply" button again
2. Refresh page (F5)
3. Clear browser cache
