## Implementation Notes

1. Each service should have a unique service code (SRV-XXXX)
2. Prices are suggested base prices and can be customized per station
3. Estimated times are averages and may vary based on vehicle condition
4. Some services (like tire replacement) don't include parts cost
5. FREE services are value-added to attract customers
6. Package deals combine multiple services at discounted rates

## Database Seeding

Recommend importing these pre-defined services during initial setup, allowing managers to:
- Enable/disable services as needed
- Adjust prices based on location
- Modify estimated times based on staff capability
- Add custom services specific to their station
